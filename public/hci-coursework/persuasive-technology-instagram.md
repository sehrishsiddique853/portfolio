# Persuasive Technology Strategies and Instagram

**Type:** Human–Computer Interaction / Persuasive Technology coursework  
**Format:** Group analysis and interface-design proposals  
**Date:** April 14, 2026  
**Collaborator:** Tatheer Zahra

## Overview

This assignment examined how Instagram's interface and features can influence user behaviour through persuasive technology strategies. It analysed examples of reduction, tunnelling, tailoring, suggestion, self-monitoring, surveillance, and conditioning.

The work also identified interaction opportunities and proposed interface changes using the persuasive strategies discussed.

## Interaction and HCI considerations

- **Reduction:** Considered how direct access to common actions and contextual notification controls can reduce steps and effort.
- **Tunnelling:** Examined guided, step-by-step flows such as account and story creation.
- **Tailoring:** Reviewed how content categories and recommendations can be adapted to a person's interests.
- **Suggestion:** Considered how timely prompts or visual cues can make relevant actions more noticeable.
- **Self-monitoring and conditioning:** Proposed a messaging streak/reminder concept to make interaction frequency visible and encourage return engagement.
- **Story-expiry awareness:** Proposed a visual indicator for stories approaching expiry to help users notice time-sensitive content.

## Design proposals

1. **Messaging streak/reminder concept:** A proposed reminder and streak indicator for conversations that have not been active for a period of time. The assignment connected this concept to self-monitoring and conditioning.
2. **Story-expiry indicator:** A proposed visual cue to make stories nearing the end of their availability more noticeable, applying suggestion and self-monitoring ideas.

## Critical design consideration

Persuasive mechanisms can influence attention and repeated use, but increased engagement is not automatically a user benefit. These proposals are coursework concepts; they were not implemented in Instagram or validated through user testing.
