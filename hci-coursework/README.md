# HCI & Interaction Design Coursework

These two academic group assignments explore human–computer interaction from complementary perspectives: inclusive service navigation and persuasive interface design.

- [CarePath CMH — Hospital Visit Planning Simulator](./carepath-cmh.md): simulated hospital-visit planning, accessibility-aware routes, context-sensitive guidance, and explanatory feedback.
- [Persuasive Technology Strategies and Instagram](./persuasive-technology-instagram.md): analysis of persuasive design strategies and proposed interface cues for messaging and story expiry.

Both are coursework concepts/analyses completed with Tatheer Zahra. They are presented as academic work, not as deployed or user-validated products.
