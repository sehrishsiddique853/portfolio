# CarePath CMH — Hospital Visit Planning Simulator

**Type:** Human–Computer Interaction / Persuasive Technology coursework  
**Format:** Group assignment and simulation concept  
**Collaborator:** Tatheer Zahra

## Overview

CarePath CMH is a proposed hospital visit-planning simulator that demonstrates how a patient's choices and circumstances before arriving at a hospital can affect the visit. The assignment models outcomes such as the recommended department, doctor availability, estimated waiting time, route, stress indicators, and likelihood of completing the visit successfully.

This is an academic simulation concept, not a deployed hospital service or a clinically validated tool.

## Interaction and HCI considerations

- **Inclusive and accessible navigation:** The scenario accounts for different patient circumstances, including wheelchair users, elderly patients, children, and people arriving with an attendant. The routing logic considers accessible options such as ramps and lifts.
- **Context-aware guidance:** First-time visitors can be directed to a helpdesk, while appointment, token, and document status influence the suggested next steps.
- **Task-oriented interaction:** A structured input flow gathers the information needed to generate a visit plan.
- **Decision support:** The simulator presents several visit-related outcomes together so that users can understand the likely consequences of their choices.
- **Explanatory feedback:** The result is intended to connect the simulated outcome to the inputs that produced it, rather than showing an unexplained result.
- **Uncertainty and stress awareness:** Waiting-time and visit-success indicators make potential friction points visible during planning.

## What the assignment explored

The project focused on modelling cause-and-effect scenarios and considering how an interface can guide people through a complex service journey. The scenarios and outcomes are simulated; they should not be interpreted as real hospital data or medical advice.
